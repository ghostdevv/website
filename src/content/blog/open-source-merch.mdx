This is a living post that I hope will document my adventures into what different merchy things we can create to give away for Open Source projects! Some things have been brought, but as much as possible I hope to make things myself and collaborate with other makers.

## Stickers

I can probably best describe stickers as the gateway drug to creating things. I love sticker culture and it's probably the thing I most want to always have on hand to share with others.

<Diginate />

### Sticker Books

The most common thing I hear from people not wanting a sticker is that they don't want or have space to put it on their laptop. Let's assume you don't want to decorate your walls with stickers, well what can you do with them? For me this is sticker books - they're made of sticker paper so you can move them anytime and they serve as a beautiful time capsule of where you've been and who you've met. I think I'm on my 5th or 6th and I'd love to make a website at some point to make them searchable.

### Creating Stickers

Aside from getting stickers from a company like <a href="https://diginate.com/" target="_blank">Diginate</a>, I've finally started the journey of making my own stickers.
